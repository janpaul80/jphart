The practice

I turn ambitious, messy ideas into software people can trust.

For more than eight years, I’ve worked across frontend, backend, product design, AI systems, and deployment. That breadth is useful because real products rarely fail inside one neat discipline. I find the constraint, make the decision, and carry it through.

Clients work with me directly. The result is less ceremony, faster feedback, and a product with a coherent point of view from its first screen to its last API call.
