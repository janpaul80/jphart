01 // Problem

Creative flow breaks when collaboration arrives late.

Video teams often edit in one tool, discuss in another, and approve in a third. Vidhart’s challenge was to make review a native part of the edit—without turning a focused creative interface into a project-management dashboard.

02 // Process

The timeline became the shared language.

The product centers every comment, revision, and approval on an exact moment in the work. A restrained interface hierarchy lets the content dominate while collaboration stays one gesture away.

Performance budgets were set early so preview, annotation, and timeline interactions stayed responsive on practical hardware.

Fewer loops.
