What this site collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to paulhartmann.dev and the services offered by Paul Hartmann through this website. It explains the handling of personal information when you browse the site, submit an inquiry, purchase a package, or use client login.

2. Information collected

Information you provide

Contact submissions may include your name, email address, company, project details, and anything else you choose to write. When you purchase a package, Stripe processes your payment details; this site receives transaction and fulfillment information but does not receive or store your full card number.

OAuth account information

If you sign in with GitHub or Google, the site receives basic profile information authorized by you, such as your name, email address, avatar, provider, and provider account identifier. This site does not receive or store your provider password.

Technical information

Hosting and security systems may process IP address, device and browser details, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs. Essential cookies are used to protect OAuth state and maintain a signed login session.

3. How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, deliver purchased services, provide secure client access, operate and protect the website, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations. Personal information is not sold.

4. Service providers

The site relies on providers that process limited information on its behalf:

  • Vercel for hosting, serverless functions, logs, and performance delivery.
  • Stripe for secure checkout, payment processing, receipts, and fraud prevention.
  • GitHub and Google when you choose them as an OAuth identity provider.
  • Email infrastructure used to deliver contact inquiries.
  • Google Fonts for website typography.

Each provider handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, contractual records, security, and applicable legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information. You may sign out to clear the active authentication cookie and can control other cookies through your browser. Requests may be sent to the address below and may require identity verification.

7. International processing and children

Providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law. This website and its commercial services are not directed to children under 16.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, providers, or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top identifies the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@paulhartmann.dev.
